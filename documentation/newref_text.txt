SYNOPSIS 
Title: Gold Finance Management System (Jewel Loan Management) 
Background: Pawn shops and gold finance companies lend money against pledged gold 
jewellery. In many shops the pledge details, interest calculations, customer records and receipts 
are still kept in paper registers, which is slow, error-prone, and hard to audit. 
Proposed System: The Gold Finance Management System is a full-stack MERN (MongoDB, 
Express.js, React, Node.js) web application that digitises the complete jewel loan life cycle: 
customer KYC, jewel valuation and pledge creation, automatic loan numbering and interest 
computation, interest-only, principal and full -settlement payments, print -ready receipts with 
QR verification, overdue tracking, reports, and audit logging. 
Key Features: 
• Dashboard showing disbursed loans, active pledges, overdue loans and interest 
revenue. 
• Loan statuses Active, Closed, Overdue and Auctioned, with customer and ornament 
photo upload. 
• Role-based access (Admin and Staff) secured with JWT and bcrypt password hashing. 
• Excel (.xlsx) report export, overdue alerts, audit logs and database backup. 
Technologies Used: React 18 with Vite and React Router (frontend); Node.js and Express.js 
(REST API); MongoDB with Mongoose (database); JSON Web Tokens, bcryptjs, Multer, 
ExcelJS and qrcode.react. 
Expected Outcome: A fast, accurate, secure and paperless system that reduces calculation 
errors, keeps a complete payment history for every pledge, and gives the owner reliable reports 
for decision making.

OBJECTIVE 
The main objective of this project is to design and develop a web -based system that manages 
gold pledge loans efficiently and replaces manual register -keeping in pawn shops and gold 
finance businesses. The specific objectives are: 
1. To maintain customer profiles with KYC details (Aadhaar / PAN), photographs and 
complete pledge history. 
2. To record pledged jewels with gross weight, net weight, stone deduction, purity (karat) 
and market valuation. 
3. To generate loan numbers and compute interest automatically using configurable rates 
and tenure. 
4. To process interest-only payments, principal deductions and full loan settlements, and 
to issue print-ready receipts with QR verification. 
5. To track overdue and maturing loans and notify the staff in time. 
6. To protect data with role -based access control, JWT authentication and encrypted 
passwords. 
7. To produce financial reports with Excel export and to keep audit logs and backups for 
transparency and safety. 
SCOPE 
The system is intended for pawn shops, jewel loan companies and gold finance businesses that 
need a reliable digital record of their lending activity. 
Scope of the Project 
• Customer and KYC management with photo upload and pledge history. 
• Gold loan creation with multi -item jewel breakdown, stone deduction, purity 
assessment, market valuation, and loan status tracking (Active, Partially Paid, Closed) 
with jewel custody status (Pledged / Released). 
• Payment handling: interest -only, principal deduction and full settlement, with a 
payment ledger per loan and printable receipts. 
• Dashboard, reports (active loans, interest collections, overdue accounts, closed pledges) 
and one-click Excel export. 
• Overdue notifications, global search across loans and customers. 
• Staff management with Admin and Staff roles, audit logs, backup and export. 
• Configurable shop profile and business settings (interest rate, tenure, currency symbol, 
receipt terms). 
Limitations 
• The system works as a web application and needs a running Node.js server and a 
MongoDB database.

PROJECT DESCRIPTION 
1. Overview 
The Gold Finance Management System (Jewel Loan Management) is a MERN -stack 
application. The React frontend communicates with an Express.js REST API, which stores data 
in MongoDB through Mongoose. When a customer pledges jewellery, the staff register the 
customer, enter the jewel details, and the system creates a loan with an automatically generated 
loan number. Later payments are recorded against the loan until it is closed or, in case of 
default, auctioned.

6. Working of the System 
1. The user logs in; the server verifies the credentials and returns a JWT used for all further 
requests. 
2. The staff register the customer and the KYC details, or select an existing customer. 
3. The pledged jewels, weights, purity and valuation are entered and the loan is created 
with a unique loan number. 
4. Payments (interest, principal or full settlement) are recorded against the loan and a 
receipt is printed. 
5. Loans that cross the due date are shown as overdue; reports can be viewed and exported 
to Excel. 
6. Every sensitive action is written to the audit log; the Admin can back up the database. 
DATABASE DESIGN 
The system uses MongoDB, a document database (database: gold_finance). Data is organized 
into 7 collections validated through Mongoose ODM 8.x. Compound indexes ensure multi -
branch isolation and rapid customer/loan retrieval. Jewel items are embedded as an array inside 
each Loan document.

CONCLUSION AND FURTHER ENHANCEMENTS 
Conclusion 
The Gold Finance Management System (Jewel Loan Management) successfully replaces 
cumbersome manual paper registers with an automated, robust, and paperless MERN -stack 
architecture. By providing a centralized digital vault for customer KYC, precise ornament  
valuation (calculating net weight from gross and stone deductions), automated interest 
computation across calendar months with grace period logic, flexible repayment handling 
(interest-only, partial capital reduction, and full settlement), print -ready QR-verified receipts,

application eliminates manual calculation discrepancies and operational friction. It provides

Further Enhancements 
• Automated Multi-Channel Notifications: Direct integration with SMS and WhatsApp 
Business APIs to dispatch automated due date reminders, grace period notifications,

• Integrated Digital Payment Gateway: Counter and remote digital repayments powered

• Biometric Authentication & Digital KYC: Hardware-assisted fingerprint scanning and

Multi-Branch Enterprise Cloud Synchronization: Centralized headquarters management 
allowing seamless multi-branch data synchronization, cross-branch pledge lookups, and
